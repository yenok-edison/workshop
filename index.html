Unleash Your Voice

A immersive masterclass with Deirdre

Discover the power of your voice in an intensive, eight-hour masterclass designed for singers and music teachers.

Explore vocal technique, teaching methods and performance skills, from posture and breath support to warm-ups, diction, projection and healthy belting for pop and Broadway.

Learn how to select age-appropriate repertoire and guide young singers aged 6–9. The session also includes personalised one-on-one workshopwith Deirdre on a song of your choice, audience engagement techniques and an interactive Q&A.

What you will take away

  • Covers vocal technique, teaching methods, and live performance.
  • 1-on-1 Coaching: Personal guidance with Deirdre on a song of your choice.
  • Teaching Young Singers: Specific strategies for working with kids aged 6-9.
  • Master healthy pop/broadway belting and fatigue prevention.
