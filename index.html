Unleash Your Voice

Timing10:00 am – 6:00 pm

Elevate your singing and instruction with this intensive 8-hour masterclass covering Technique, Teaching Technique, and Performance. Discover foundational mechanics, including posture, core breath support, vocal warm-ups, diction, voice projection, healthy belting for pop and Broadway, and vocal longevity. Learn specialized pedagogy to choose age-appropriate repertoire and guide young singers aged 6–9. Cap off the session with personalized one-on-one coaching with Deirdre on a song of your choice, audience engagement training, and an interactive Q&A.
